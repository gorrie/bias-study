# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — contributors and maintainers — pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Focusing on what is technically true, reproducible, and well-sourced.
- Disagreeing with methods and conclusions on the merits, with evidence.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully, and giving it civilly.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks unrelated to the technical question at hand.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

This is a project about measuring political-framing bias. Contributions are judged on
reproducibility and evidence, never on the contributor's own politics.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in
public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or
contacting the maintainers via the repository. Maintainers will review and respond, and may
remove, edit, or reject contributions or comments that violate this Code of Conduct.
